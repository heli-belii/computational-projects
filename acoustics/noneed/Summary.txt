We learned some little things, for example (1). how to read and write .WAV files, or at least find the code to do so and (2). how to use the Makerspace AV studio equipment, but also a larger lesson surrounding the fallability of a process. When recording the GarageBand MIDI notes, we realized what we're pretty sure GarageBand called Bb3 (we initially thought it was calling it a Bb2) was familiar to Helios as a Bb4. Initially, we stuck with Helios's convention but after investigating the FFT we realized that either there had to be a mysterious octave undertone or we were off by an octave, the latter of which we took as our conclusion. This was a relatively minor fix, but it demonstrated the importance of having an understanding and some level of trust in our work and the results it produced. This is not to promote complete trust in everything we code, but it helped us recognize there is room for mistakes in our inputs in addition to our code, and that sometimes we need to trust what we have. On the topic of understanding, we had a moment where simply copying over code from class left us with an incorrect result, and it took an understanding of what the code was actually doing (more specifically, what the role each input of the function was) to get code functioning as needed. Overall, this project taught us a lot about the importance of maintaining a working understanding of each of the elements of our project and how they communicate to provide us with our results.
